ALPHA AI

A study companion for Ethiopian students: past exams, books, AI help, quizzes and notes, in English and Amharic.

Download for Android (APK) Android phones only. Always download from this page.

How to install

  1. Tap the download button and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android says installing from this source is blocked, tap Settings and allow it for your browser or file manager.
  3. Tap Install, then open ALPHA AI.
  4. Choose the free level, a 5-minute trial, or a paid level. The app tells you how to pay and what to enter.

The app tells you when a new version is ready. To update, download it again from this page and install it over the old one.
