ALPHA AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

ALPHA AI (ALPHA Educational Hub) is a study app for students, developed by Kinfemichael. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

No accounts

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not run a server that stores your personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your phone.

Information stored on your phone

Your nickname (optional), settings, chats, saved chats, quizzes, notes, quiz progress, downloaded books and AI models, and your points balance are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time from the app, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

AI chat, quizzes and notes

Offline AI: when you use a model downloaded to your phone, your text is processed on the phone and is not sent anywhere.

Online AI: when you choose an online provider (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek, Together AI or a custom address), the app sends your messages and the recent conversation to that provider using the API key you entered. The provider processes the data under its own privacy policy. Your API key is stored only on your phone and is sent only to the provider you selected. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into AI chats.

Downloads

When you download books, exam files or AI models, or when the app refreshes its list of resources, it connects to public hosting services such as Hugging Face and GitHub. Those services may see your IP address as in any web request.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to show and measure ads, according to Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings. You can earn points by watching optional rewarded ads and use them to pause ads for a short time or to speed up offline AI.

Permissions

The app uses the Internet and network-state permissions to reach AI providers, download files and decide whether to show ads. It opens files you choose (such as a model file) through the Android file picker, and opens phone, email or Telegram only when you tap a contact button.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
