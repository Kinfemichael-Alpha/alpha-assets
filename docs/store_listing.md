Full description:
ALPHA AI is a study companion built for Ethiopian students.

- AI tutor: ask any question and get step-by-step explanations. Use your own online AI key (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq and more) or download a small AI model and work offline.
- Past exams: download Ethiopian national exams by subject and year, take them in the app, see your score and read explanations.
- Generated questions and notes: fresh practice questions and short study notes for each subject, ready to download.
- Quizzes: generate multiple-choice or true/false questions on any topic and track your progress.
- Read text from photos: take or pick a picture of a page or question and the app reads the text for you, in English and Amharic.
- Translation: translate answers between English and Amharic on your phone with a downloadable translation model.
- Books: browse and download textbooks by grade and subject.
- Private by design: no account needed, your chats, notes and progress stay on your phone.
- Earn points by watching optional ads and use them to pause ads or speed up offline AI.

AI answers can contain mistakes; check important facts with your teacher or textbook.
