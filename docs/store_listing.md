Full description:
ALPHA AI is a study companion built for Ethiopian students.

- AI tutor: ask any question and get step-by-step explanations. Use your own online AI key (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq and more) or download a small AI model and work offline.
- Quizzes: generate multiple-choice or true/false questions on any topic and track your progress.
- Short notes: turn a topic into clear study notes you can save and read later.
- Books and exams: browse and download textbooks and past exams by grade and subject.
- Private by design: no account needed, your chats and notes stay on your phone.
- Earn points by watching optional ads and use them to pause ads or speed up offline AI.

Data safety form answers:
- Data collected by the app itself: none. No account, no developer server.
- Data shared: advertising ID and device/usage data are collected by Google AdMob (third party, for advertising).
- Data sent to user-chosen AI provider: messages the user types (user-initiated, with the user's own key).
- Data encrypted in transit: yes (HTTPS).
- Users can request deletion: all data is local; uninstall or clear data.
- Target audience: 13+ (ads not child-directed). Contains ads: yes.
