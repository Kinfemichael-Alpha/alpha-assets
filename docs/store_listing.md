## Full description (English)
ALPHA AI is a study companion built for Ethiopian students.

- AI tutor: ask any question and get step-by-step explanations. Use your own online AI key (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq and more), download a small AI model and work offline, or use the highest level where we provide the AI for you.
- Past exams: browse Ethiopian national exams by subject and year. Higher levels let you take them in the app, see your score and read explanations.
- Generated questions and notes: fresh practice questions and short study notes for each subject.
- Quizzes: generate multiple-choice or true/false questions on any topic and track your progress.
- Read text from photos: take or pick a picture of a page or question and the app reads the text, in English and Amharic.
- Translation: translate between English and Amharic on your phone with a downloadable model.
- Books: browse and download textbooks by grade and subject.
- Levels: start free, try the app for 5 minutes, or unlock more with a paid level. One account works on one phone at a time.
- The free level shows ads; the highest level has none.

AI answers can contain mistakes; check important facts with your teacher or textbook.

## Data safety answers (changed since the first version: the app now has accounts)
- Collects or shares data? Yes.
- Personal info: phone number (account, collected, not shared, purpose: account management and app functionality).
- Financial info: payment details entered by the user (CBE sender name, last 4 digits, amount, or Telebirr transaction ID). Collected, not shared, purpose: account management. Payment itself is made outside the app, by bank or Telebirr.
- Device or other IDs: a code derived from the Android ID (account-to-phone binding) and the advertising ID (AdMob, advertising).
- App interactions and diagnostics: collected by the AdMob SDK for advertising.
- Messages typed to an online AI provider: sent only when the user selects one with their own key; on the highest level they pass through our server to the provider and are not stored.
- Photos: processed on the phone; not collected.
- Encrypted in transit: Yes (HTTPS).
- Deletion: users can request deletion of the server account and payment record by email; local data is deleted in the app or by uninstalling.
- Account creation: phone number registration for trial and paid levels; the free level needs none.
